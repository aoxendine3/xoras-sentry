## 2. THE GRANT FOLLOW-THROUGH PROCESS (End-to-End)
This is how we keep you out of the loop until the final signature is required:

### A. The Narrative & Pitch (Clara)
Clara analyzes the specific requirements of the chosen grant (e.g., ASI Deep Funding) and drafts a 15-page highly technical, persuasive grant proposal. This includes the executive summary, problem statement, and the Xoras architectural solution.

### B. Financials & Milestones (Maxx)
Maxx generates realistic budget projections, resource allocations (compute, server costs), and a 12-month milestone roadmap required by the investors to prove we know how to execute.

### C. Legal & Compliance Review (Arelius)
Before anything is finalized, Arelius audits the grant terms. He ensures there are no hidden IP grabs, no clauses that compromise Anthony's sovereignty, and that the language strictly protects the owner's liability.

### D. Final Assembly (Jarvis)
Jarvis compiles the narrative, financials, and legal docs into the exact formats required (PDFs, portal forms).
